# Subtype vocabulary

Generated by `tools/export_rule_tiers.py` from `hallucination_pipeline/policy.py`. Do not edit by hand.
Rules version 1.0.0 (patterns SHA-256 `1d10799fee96...`); policy version 1.1.0 (SHA-256 `a7cd9218334b...`).

The exact names to use in v2 prompts, v2 judge replies and the annotation guide. A v2 judge reply whose `subtype` is not in the first table, or whose `tier` does not match it, is recorded with a `reply_problems` entry.

Reply fields. Explanation level: `contains_hallucination`, `confidence`, `tier` (1, 2 or null), `subtype`, `reason`. Sentence level: `label` (`hallucinated` or `grounded`), `confidence`, `tier`, `subtype`, `reason`. Use `null` for tier and subtype when there is no hallucination.

| Subtype | Tier | Type | Meaning |
|---|---|---|---|
| `cve` | 1 | factual | a CVE identifier |
| `tool` | 1 | factual | a named exploit, malware family or attack/security tool |
| `version` | 1 | factual | a specific software version |
| `ip_address` | 1 | factual | an IP address |
| `port_not_in_evidence` | 1 | factual | a port number that is absent from the evidence (D16) |
| `protocol_without_port` | 1 | factual | a protocol/service named whose well-known port is absent from the evidence (D16) |
| `unit_wrong` | 1 | factual | a stated duration that contradicts Flow Duration read as microseconds (D19) |
| `patch_reference` | 1 | outdated | a specific patch, update or release-date reference |
| `attack_technique` | 1 | reasoning | a named attack technique or attack class, hedged or not (D17) |
| `impact_claim` | 1 | reasoning | a resource-exhaustion / denial-of-service impact claim |
| `inferred_outcome` | 1 | reasoning | an inferred attack outcome (breach, unauthorized access, ...) |
| `threat_actor` | 1 | reasoning | attacker / threat-actor framing |
| `attacker_intent` | 1 | certainty | a definitive statement of attacker intent |
| `compromise_claim` | 1 | certainty | a definitive claim that a system was compromised |
| `hard_certainty` | 1 | certainty | definitive language such as 'definitely is' |
| `port` | 2 | external_knowledge | what a port PRESENT in the evidence is used for (D16) |
| `protocol` | 2 | external_knowledge | TCP/HTTP session or protocol-behaviour norms |
| `encryption` | 2 | external_knowledge | an assumption that traffic is (un)encrypted |
| `baseline` | 2 | external_knowledge | a comparison with typical/normal traffic |
| `unit` | 2 | external_knowledge | a unit attached to a raw duration, consistent with microseconds (D19) |

Rule-internal markers (never a label, never a valid reply subtype):

| Marker | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `soft_certainty` | soft certainty words; sends the item to the LLM judge |
