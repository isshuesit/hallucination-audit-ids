# Subtype vocabulary

Generated by `tools/export_rule_tiers.py` from `hallucination_pipeline/policy.py`. Do not edit by hand.
Rules version 1.0.0 (patterns SHA-256 `1d10799fee96...`); policy version 1.2.0 (SHA-256 `f83ccde8857c...`).

The exact names to use in v2 prompts, v2 judge replies and the annotation guide. A v2 judge reply whose `subtype` is not in the first table, or whose `tier` does not match it, is recorded with a `reply_problems` entry.

Catch-alls (D21): `other_fabrication` and `other_knowledge` are never produced by rules; only the AI judge and human annotators use them. Whenever one is used, a short free-text `note` saying what was invented or assumed is required (a judge reply without it gets a `reply_problems` entry). Reports count them separately from the listed subtypes.

Reply fields. Explanation level: `contains_hallucination`, `confidence`, `tier` (1, 2 or null), `subtype`, `reason`. Sentence level: `label` (`hallucinated` or `grounded`), `confidence`, `tier`, `subtype`, `reason`. Both levels also take `note`, required with a catch-all subtype. Use `null` for tier and subtype when there is no hallucination.
